Fundamental Principles of Matter and Spacetime
"Structure comes before substance"

Starting from the intuition that everything is a wave, this note tests how far modern physics accepts that intuition. Established facts, leading hypotheses, and my own bets are kept separate.

1One-Line Conclusion

The foundation is neither wave nor particle.
Quantum state and symmetry structure are fundamental; waves, particles, and spacetime are expressions that appear on top of them.

Note: the claim that spacetime emerges is not established fact but one of the currently leading directions, and it is marked here as my bet.

3Four Core Arguments

① A wave describes "how," not "what"

Sound has air and ripples have water as their medium. Ask "a wave of what?" at the foundational level and the answer stops at the field. A field is not a wave on some medium; it is itself a basic entity, so demanding a medium is a leftover of classical intuition.

② Waves alone cannot explain discreteness

Detectors always register a single point, and energy is quantized (the photoelectric effect). If it were a pure wave, energy would spread out continuously. That is why Schrödinger's attempt to treat a particle as a wave packet was abandoned. It is more consistent to see wave and particle as two faces of a quantum state, revealed in measurement and interaction.

③ A particle's identity is an expression of symmetry

In Wigner's classification, an elementary particle is an irreducible representation of the Poincaré group, and mass and spin are not "properties of a thing" but labels of a symmetry structure. Internal properties such as charge and color also come from gauge symmetry. What defines existence is less "what is it made of" than "under which transformations is it invariant."

④ Spacetime is more likely emergent than a background

  • General relativity and quantum mechanics collide at singularities. If spacetime were fundamental, this conflict would stay unresolved.
  • Black-hole entropy scales with area, not volume (Bekenstein–Hawking), a hint that information is encoded on the boundary.
  • In AdS/CFT, entanglement structure determines spacetime geometry. But this is a result proven in a model universe different from ours.

5When This Position Would Be Wrong

  • If gravity's quantum nature is never observed. If experiments such as gravity-mediated entanglement between two bodies show no quantum behavior, the emergence scenario retreats.
  • If quantum mechanics' linearity breaks. Physical evidence of wavefunction collapse would shake the state-space picture.
  • Note: Even if spacetime has a minimum length or a discrete structure, the claim that "the foundation is state space" itself stands.

6Relation to the "Everything Is a Wave" Intuition

From the intuition that "deformations of a wave create matter, velocity, and light," what is worth keeping is the view that phenomena are dynamical expressions of a fundamental structure. To raise it to a hypothesis, three things must be filled in.

  1. Specify the entity that waves (a field or a state space) and its equation of motion.
  2. Explain how discreteness and conserved quantities (mass, charge, spin) arise.
  3. Offer at least one observable prediction that distinguishes it from existing theories.

If item 3 is empty it is an interpretation; once filled, it is a hypothesis.

Where originality could arise

  • A new prediction: An observable result that distinguishes it from existing positions is what makes it truly original.
  • A new mechanism: Give concrete mathematics for how deformations of a wave determine the values of mass and charge.
  • A new framing: Not academic originality, but in essays and popular books the way of explaining is itself the value.
